What Is All-Star Team-Up?

All-Star Team-Up 2K Arena runs the Private All-Star Team Up game mode — here's exactly how it works.

🔒

Private Locker Room

The higher-seeded captain creates the Locker Room and owns it — they invite the other 9 players and set it to Private before anyone joins.

🎮

Locked Settings

Host sets All-Star Team Up mode, East vs West, Hall of Fame difficulty, 5-minute quarters. Screenshot the settings before tip-off — required with every result.

🏀

Real NBA All-Stars Only

Every player picks a position and a real NBA All-Star — any era, old school to new school. No made-up players, no non-All-Stars.

⚖️

Fair Competition

Solos are balanced onto teams by skill level. Same rules, same settings, same standard for every match — no exceptions, no shortcuts.

How It Works

Simple. Fair. Competitive.

01

Pick Your Tournament

Choose your game (2K25 or 2K26) and your console (PS4, PS5, Xbox One, or Series X|S). Each tournament has its own bracket and $450 prize pool ($300 / $100 / $50).

02

Register & Pay

Teams pay $50 for all 5 players. Solo players pay $15 and get placed on a balanced team by skill level. Payment via Stripe — secure and instant.

03

Round Robin First

Every team plays 3 group stage games. Your record earns your seed. Best W-L record = top seed. Tiebreaker is point differential.

04

Double Elimination Bracket

All 16 teams enter the bracket seeded by group stage record. Lose once → Losers Bracket. Lose twice → eliminated. Grand Finals broadcast live.
