Official Rules

Locker Room Setup

The higher-seeded team captain creates the locker room and invites the opposing captain. Before tip-off, screenshot the settings and submit with the result. Required settings — no exceptions:

🏀 Mode: All-Star Team Up
🔒 Privacy: Private
⚠️ Difficulty: Hall of Fame
⏱ Quarter Length: 5 Minutes
🗺 Teams: West vs East
🎮 Platform: Xbox One Only
01

Registration & Payment

Teams pay $50 (captain pays once for all 5). Solo players pay $15 each. Spots confirmed after payment only. No refunds after bracket seeding.

02

Roster Rules

Each team must have exactly 5 registered players plus 1 optional substitute registered before the bracket is released.

03

Age Requirement

All players must be 18+. Players 13–17 may compete with written parental consent submitted at registration.

04

Match Format

Round Robin (3 games) determines seeding. Double Elimination bracket follows — best of 3, best of 5 in Grand Finals.

05

Scheduling

Captains have 48 hours to coordinate match times after bracket release. All scheduling confirmed in #scheduling. All times ET.

06

No Cheating

Exploits, hacks, macros, or outside assistance = immediate disqualification and permanent ban. First offense is final.

07

Reporting Results

Winning captain posts a screenshot/score in #results within 1 hour of match completion. Both captains must confirm.

08

Prize Payout

Paid to winning captain via PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App within 7 days of Finals. 30-day claim window.
